    Before you start

    How to play

    • Objective: Complete as many warehouse shifts as you can in 60 seconds. Each full shift has two jobs: pick the right items, then plot the dock route.
    • Memory: Two items flash on the loading dock for 2 seconds. Then four cards appear. Select both items that were on the flash — pick two cards first, then the pair is checked together. If you miss, you retry the same items until both are correct. The clock keeps running.
    • Route: Draw the forklift route from Dock 1 to Dock 2, then 3, then 4 on a 4×4 floor. Move only up, down, left, or right. Do not reuse a tile and do not enter blocked shelves. The route must cover every open tile. Tap tiles one by one, or press and drag/swipe a continuous line through them — fastest on a phone. If you tap a dock out of order it is rejected and the route stays as drawn — nothing is erased, so you keep your progress.
    • Scoring: Score starts at 0. Every completed shift awards 10 points. Finish a shift with a perfect memory pick (both items right the first try) and your Perfect streak grows: streak ×2 pays 20 points, ×3 pays 30, ×4 pays 40, and so on. A wrong pick resets the streak, so the next round counts from 1 again and pays just the 10. Incomplete work when time hits 0 awards nothing, and the score freezes.
    • Penalties: There is no point deduction. A wrong item pair wastes time: the same shipment flashes again, the streak resets, and no points are awarded until both the memory pick and the full dock route are completed. Unused time at 0 is lost.
    • Duration: The countdown starts at 60 only after Start. The game stops automatically at 0.
    • Controls: Click or tap cards; tap or drag across floor tiles to draw the route. Arrow keys or WASD extend the route. Backspace or Z undoes the last tile. M mutes sound. Tab and Enter work on buttons.
    • Power-ups: ⚡ Express tiles advance the forklift two tiles in one move. 💡 Reveal tiles flash the safest next tile so you don’t paint yourself into a dead end. Power-ups award no extra points — only the streak scoring counts.
    • Perfect streaks: Finish a shift with a correct memory pair on the first try and you build a 🔥 Perfect streak, shown in the header. Each streak level adds +10 to the shift: ×2 pays 20, ×3 pays 30, and so on.

    The 60-second clock is still running. Return to the floor to keep playing.
